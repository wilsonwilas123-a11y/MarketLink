# MarketLink Evaluation Demo Script

Target length: 4–6 minutes. Record in a desktop browser at 1280×800 or wider so the map and catalogue layouts are visible. Use only the isolated demo accounts and seeded data from `README.md`; do not expose `.env` files or real credentials.

## Scenes

1. **Landing page (20 sec)** — Show the farm hero, search, market links and fresh-produce section. Say: “MarketLink connects local farmers with shoppers and reserves orders for pickup.”
2. **Market discovery (35 sec)** — Open Markets. Show Lagos markets, weekly trading days, open status, list/map view and the market details/roster. Point out that mapped outside markets are labeled as unlisted and photo sources are credited.
3. **Products (40 sec)** — Search and filter by category, market day, market and price; open a product detail. Show the matching pickup market and that category artwork is used where a listing photo is unavailable.
4. **Farmer profile (25 sec)** — Open a farmer page. Show profile details, products, market schedule, pickup pin/map and reviews.
5. **Pickup cart and checkout (45 sec)** — Add products from one farmer/market to the cart, select a pickup date and time, and place the reservation. Explain that payment happens in person at pickup; do not attempt an online payment.
6. **Order tracking (30 sec)** — Open the customer order history and order detail. Show status milestones and, if the demo order is still before cutoff, demonstrate the edit/cancel controls without changing any non-demo data.
7. **Saved markets and repeat pickup (20 sec)** — Save a market with the heart, find it under Saved places & produce, then use Buy again on a completed order to show that only currently available items return to the cart.
8. **Customer dashboard (25 sec)** — Show recent orders, favorites and notifications. The optional AI assistant is not needed for the core workflow.
9. **Farmer dashboard (45 sec)** — Sign in with a demo farmer. Show stall details, market days and pin, product photo upload control, current-week quantity and recurring weekly stock. Show order status controls and weekly performance summary.
10. **Restock alert (20 sec)** — If demo data is prepared, favorite a product, mark it sold out in the farmer dashboard, then restore quantity and show the in-app notification. Avoid modifying shared or production accounts.
11. **Admin dashboard (45 sec)** — Sign in as the demo admin. Show market/category tools, farmer approval, account controls, listing/review moderation, announcements and pickup reports.
12. **Contact page (15 sec)** — Show the team’s approved public contact details and the map pin configured for evaluation.

## Capture checklist

- Use a clean browser profile or hide personal bookmarks, history and notifications.
- Use the accounts and demo password in the repository README, then sign out between roles.
- Keep the browser URL bar visible for page context, but never show terminal output containing environment variables.
- Do not enable browser location permission during the recording; the market list still works by city and day.
- Do not present seeded demo data as live inventory from a real farmer.
