1.1 Background and Necessity for the Web Application
Local farmers markets are growing in popularity as shoppers look for fresh, seasonal, and
locally grown produce. However, customers rarely know in advance which Farmers will
be at a market on a given day, what stock they have, or at what price.
Availability is usually communicated informally through chalkboards, printed flyers, or
word of mouth. This means customers often arrive to find popular items already sold out
or make a trip only to discover a Farmer is closed for the week.
Farmers, in turn, have no easy way to publicize their weekly inventory, take pre-orders
ahead of market day, or build a lasting relationship with regular customers. A full-stack
Web application is required to bring Farmers and customers together on a single
platform. Through this platform, Farmers can publish their weekly stock, pricing, and
manage incoming pre-orders.
Additionally, customers can discover nearby markets, view market and Farmer locations
through an integrated map using Google Maps API or OpenStreetMap, browse what is
available, reserve items for pickup, and leave feedback. Centralizing this information
reduces wasted trips, helps Farmers plan their harvest and stock better, and strengthens
the connection between local producers and their community.

1.2 Proposed Solution
The proposed full-stack Web application titled ‘MarketLink’ aims to provide a unified
platform that connects local farmers-market Farmers with customers. Farmers can
register, list their weekly stock and prices, manage pre-orders placed by customers for
pickup at the market, and provide accurate stall or pickup locations that can be
displayed through Google Maps API or OpenStreetMap.
Some of the actions that customers can perform:
• Browse markets and Farmers near them
• Search and filter available products
• Use Google Maps API or OpenStreetMap to
locate nearby markets and Farmers, view
directions, and identify pickup points
• Place pre-orders for pickup
• Track their order history
• Save preferent Farmers and products
• Leave reviews and ratings
The platform maintains a database of Farmer inventories, customer order history, and
favorites to help customers quickly find items. Along with an optional basic AI feature,
they can get answers to common questions such as market timings, Farmer availability,
and product details. By combining inventory management, pre-ordering, and optional
basic AI intelligent assistance in one system, MarketLink makes shopping at local farmers
markets more convenient, predictable, and personal.

1.4 Scope of Project
This project involves the development of a comprehensive full-stack Web-based
application that connects local farmers-market Farmers with customers. The application
allows Farmers to register their stall, list their weekly stock and prices, manage pre-orders,
and map their stall or pickup location using Google Maps API or OpenStreetMap.
Customers can create accounts, browse markets and Farmers, search and filter
products, view map-based market and Farmer locations, place or cancel pre-orders for
pickup, save favorite Farmers/products, and leave reviews and ratings.
The platform maintains a database of Farmer inventories, customer order history, and
favorites. It supports secure registration, role-based access, and interactive dashboards
for both Farmers and customers. An optional basic AI assistant/chatbot is included that
helps customers search for items and provides answers to frequently asked questions.
An administrator role takes care of the elevated management such as managing users,
report generation, and so on.
The Web solution is intended to be responsive, intuitive, and scalable, ensuring smooth
communication between Farmers and customers, accurate stock visibility, and a
convenient pre-order and pickup experience within a single platform.

1.5 Constraints
The development of the ‘MarketLink’ Web application must adhere to several
constraints to ensure its successful implementation and operation. Technically, the
application must be compatible with major Web browsers and should be responsive
across various devices. You might encounter constraints related to data storage, data
synchronization, and backup procedures. Usage of various images and videos can be
subject to licensing agreements and copyright restrictions. It is important to understand
and comply with these constraints to avoid legal issues.

The Web application will not have any functionality for payment gateways – payment
for pre-orders is settled in person at pickup. Delivery/courier logistics are out of scope;
the application only supports pickup at the market. Verification of Farmer’s identity,
licensing, or organic/food-safety certification will not be a part of the Web application
functionality.
1.6 Functional Requirements
Application will be designed with a set of forms/pages with menus representing the
choice of activities to be performed.
Customer Features
User Registration and Login
• Customers must be able to register, log in, and securely access their dashboard.
• Name, contact number, e-mail ID, and address must be supplied during
registration.
• Customers can save multiple favorite Farmers and products.
• Sharing of accounts among family members can be permitted (optional).
Browse Markets and Farmers
• Customers can browse nearby farmers markets by location and day and view the
list of Farmers present at each market.
• Customers can view a Farmer’s profile including stall name, location, operating
days, and current weekly stock.
• Customers can view markets and Farmer stalls on an embedded map powered
by Google Maps API or OpenStreetMap, with location markers and directions to
the selected pickup point.
Search, Browse, and Filter Products
• Customers can browse product categories (vegetables, fruits, dairy, baked
goods, and so on) with filters for price, category, market, and day.
• Customers can view product details including price, unit, quantity available, and
Farmer.

Farmer Features
Farmer Registration and Profile Management
• At the time of registration, Farmers must provide stall/business name, contact

• Farmers can view and optionally respond to customer reviews left on their
products.
Admin Features

1.7 Non-Functional Requirements
There are several non-functional requirements that should be fulfilled by the application.
They include:
• Safe to use: The application should not result in any malicious downloads or
unnecessary file downloads.
• Accessibility: The application should have clear and legible fonts, user-interface
elements, and navigation elements.
• User-friendliness: The application should be easy to navigate with clear menus
and other elements and easy to understand.
• Operability: The application should be reliable and efficient.
• Performance: The application should demonstrate high value of performance
through speed and throughput. In simple terms, the application should have
minimal load time and smooth page redirection, even when browsing large
product catalogues.
• Scalability: The application architecture and infrastructure should be designed
to handle increasing numbers of Farmers, customers, and product listings,
especially during peak market days.
• Security: The application should implement adequate security measures such as
authentication. For example, only registered users can access certain features.
• Availability: The application should be available 24/7 with minimum downtime,
so customers can browse and pre-order at any time before pickup.
• Compatibility: The application should be compatible with latest browsers and
various devices.
IMPORTANT:
These are the bare minimum expectations from the project. It is a must to implement
the FUNCTIONAL and NON-FUNCTIONAL requirements given in this SRS. Once they are
complete, you can use your own creativity and imagination to add more features if
required.
